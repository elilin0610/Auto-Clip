## False positives in the first rules (fixed)

The initial rules suggested one incorrect clip in A; all matches in A were false positives.

| OCR text | What it was | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| `Team Eliminated +5 RP` (top right) | Ranked RP notification for a different squad | Require uppercase English cues, with only icons before the keyword |
| `Announcer: Kill Leader eliminated.` | Subtitle | Same |
| `Knockdown Shield` (14 times) | Item name while downed, incorrectly fuzzy-matched to KNOCKED DOWN | Same; also exclude SHIELD |
| `SQUAD ELIMINATED` (large text at top) | Your own squad was eliminated | Only accept `SQUAD WIPE +100` for wiping another squad |

After the fix, A returned no clips. The top-right HUD showed 569 damage and no kill count; RP went from −20 to 0 from placement. There likely were no player knockdowns or eliminations, so zero clips was the expected result.

Additional cues in the SteelSeries footage:

- `ASSIST, KNOCKED DOWN …`, `ASSIST, ELIMINATION …`, and `ENEMY SQUAD ELIMINATED +50`: teammate-led action, counted as assists (0.5 weight). No suggestion for assist-only fights.
- `COMEBACK KILL +300`: counted as an elimination.

## Misses and false positives (final rules)

The source notes refer to ten SteelSeries clips and record these outcomes:
- Eight clips each produced one suggestion, visually checked for a player KNOCKED DOWN or ELIMINATED cue.
- One assist-only clip (four assists) and one post-death spectator clip were excluded.
- The notes also list a clip with a single knock at 0:56; the fight continued past the recording. Its 1.0 score was below threshold, a deliberate conservative choice rather than an OCR miss.

OCR often misses the first 0.5–1 seconds of a cue fading in. Cues last 2–3 seconds and sampling is twice per second; no complete misses from that cause have been seen.

## Import to export (audio/video sync)

NVENC exports were fully decoded to check for errors. Audio and video were separately matched against the source to measure offsets.

| Test | Interval | Result | Relative A/V offset (start / middle / end) |
|---|---|---|---|
| A manually selected | 9:10–9:55 | 1920×1080, 45.03 s, full decode without errors | −5 / −5 / −5 ms |
| B adjusted suggestion | 0:15–1:00 (original 0:00–1:01) | 1920×1080, 45.03 s, full decode without errors | −21 / +13 / −21 ms (one frame at 30fps = 33 ms) |
| MKV input | 0:20–0:59 | 1920×1080, 39.04 s, full decode without errors | −23 / +10 / −22 ms |
| 16:10 source (not Apex, aspect ratio test) | 15:00–15:20 | 2134×1200, frame centered with black padding | Audio −21 ms constant |

Offsets stayed within one video frame and did not drift toward the end. The approximately 21 ms fixed offset is AAC encoder priming (1024 samples), automatically compensated by the player. The original A/B file SHA-256, byte sizes, and modification times stayed unchanged after export.

## Traditional Chinese game UI (viewed online, not downloaded)

A short Traditional Chinese Apex video by fakolli was examined frame by frame in the browser; the app processed a screenshot.

- A knock cue appears below the crosshair as “**擊倒** name +150” with “造成 157 點傷害” on the next line. OCR produced “擊 倒 我 請 + 巧 0”; it was inaccurate on the red username but recognized the leading knock text.
- zh-Hant was detected automatically, also using the bottom-left HUD label “下個等級：N 進化”.
- The subtitle “擊破了敵人的防護罩。” was not a false positive.
- Chinese has no uppercase/lowercase distinction, so keywords are required at the start of a line to reject subtitles.

Chinese elimination, squad-wipe, kill-leader, assist and champion cues have not been seen and remain guesses. Only one screenshot was checked; no full-match miss/false-positive rates were measured.
